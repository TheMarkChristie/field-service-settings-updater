# League Compliance & Streaming Rights — checklist (get it in writing)

1. Ownership structure: notify the league/association of the fan
   ownership model and any threshold rules on ownership changes; the
   75% identity-ballot process (name/badge) requires league
   ratification — build league sign-off into the identity-change
   runbook before announcing results as final.
2. STREAMING (the promise most likely to bite): "every home game
   streamed live worldwide" (P33) requires the league's written
   confirmation that clubs hold their own home streaming rights, and on
   what terms (blackouts, delay windows, away-club consent, advertising
   rights in-stream — we sell stream ads under P69). Do not put the
   promise on the checkout page until this letter exists.
3. Away audio commentary (P34): confirm accreditation/permission for a
   club commentator at away rinks.
4. Club licensing: facility, medical, and coaching-qualification
   requirements tracked on the commitments calendar.
5. Player registration and transfer windows: executive-owned; fans vote
   strategy and budgets (P45), never registrations themselves — keep
   the platform's ballot templates worded accordingly.
