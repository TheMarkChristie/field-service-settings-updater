# Financial Controls Policy — DRAFT for board adoption

1. Bank mandate: two authorised signatories required for any payment
   over £500; the platform's £5,000 unbudgeted-spend ballot gate (P24)
   governs the DECISION, this policy governs EXECUTION.
2. Segregation: the person raising a payment is never its sole
   approver. Share proceeds are reconciled monthly against the
   platform's share register export (WooCommerce/Stripe vs register).
3. Cards & expenses: club cards have named holders and monthly limits;
   expenses require receipts within 30 days; volunteer expenses (P72)
   are pre-approved by budget line.
4. Budget cycle: the executive drafts the season budget; the board
   reviews; the owners ratify by annual ballot (P23); quarterly
   reforecast is reported at member meetings.
5. Reporting: monthly one-page summary to owners (P21) is generated
   from the accounts, not alongside them — one source of truth.
6. Audit/independent examination: appoint an external accountant to
   prepare statutory accounts; commission an independent review of
   controls annually while turnover is below audit thresholds.
7. Reserves: hold a minimum cash reserve equal to [2] months' fixed
   costs before surplus-allocation ballots are offered to owners.
