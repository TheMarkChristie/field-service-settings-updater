# Governance & Season Calendar — Governance Officer's working doc

## Standing ballot calendar (P58)
- Season budget ratification: [May] — before season budget commitments.
- KIT VOTE: run the ballot no later than [January] — suppliers need
  final designs months before next season; working-back rule: supplier
  deadline minus 4 weeks = ballot close. A kit ballot that ignores
  procurement lead time produces a kit that arrives in November.
- Season objectives ballot: pre-season.
- Ticket pricing consultation: [February], feeding Fanbase setup.
- AGM + statutory resolutions: within [6] months of year end.

## The platform already automates
Ballot lifecycle, quorum chasing, decision register, commitments
calendar reminders (weekly show, monthly Q&A, monthly financials,
quarterly meetings, annual report, filings, insurance, safeguarding
review, SH01 allotment returns with CSV export).

## Manual rhythm the officer owns
- Load each season's fixtures as matches (rota + reporters per match).
- Quarterly: reconcile share register vs Companies House; review
  moderation and complaints themes; check credential-vault access list.
- Annually: governance rules review — any change to quorum, majorities,
  or scope goes to a 75% constitutional ballot (P75).
