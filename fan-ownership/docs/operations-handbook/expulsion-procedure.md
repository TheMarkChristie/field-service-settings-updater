# Expulsion Procedure — Checklist (DRAFT)

The end of the graduated sanctions ladder (P38, Shareholders'
Agreement clauses 4–5): warning → temporary suspension → expulsion
with share surrender. This checklist is the due process; skipping a
step exposes the club.

1. **Grounds** — identify the conduct against SHA clause 4 (violence/
   discrimination, criminal offence, serious disrepute, democracy
   manipulation, access misuse, brand misuse). Gather evidence
   (moderation records, audit log, reports).
2. **Written notice** — email the member: the allegation, the
   evidence, the clause engaged, and a response window (14 days
   recommended). Suspension of expression rights may apply meanwhile
   (clause 5); voting rights are only ever removed at expulsion.
3. **Response** — log anything received; extend once for good cause.
4. **Board decision** — a board vote in the Board Workspace (conflicts
   declared, recusals applied). The outcome is auto-minuted.
5. **Execution** — on expulsion: surrender all shares (register reason
   `expelled`, no payout), close platform access, revoke sessions and
   app tokens, void unredeemed gift codes they purchased.
6. **Record** — audit log entry; anonymised note in the annual report
   (SHA clause 5); retain the evidence bundle per the retention policy.
7. **No appeal is promised** in the current draft — solicitor to
   confirm whether an appeal step is required for enforceability.
