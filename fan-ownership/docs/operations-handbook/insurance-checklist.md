# Insurance Checklist — broker instructions

1. Directors' & Officers' (D&O): ESSENTIAL and unusual here — disclose
   to the broker that member ballots bind directors and that fans hold
   confidence votes on appointments (P45). Cover the board, the
   Governance Officer role, and claims arising from ballot-driven
   decisions.
2. Public liability: matchday crowds, volunteers at the rink, events.
3. Player accident & travel: league-mandated minimums plus club top-up.
4. Employers' liability: statutory, covers staff and applies to
   volunteers in practice — confirm volunteers are named as covered.
5. Cyber & media: the club holds member personal data, payment flows,
   and broadcasts content worldwide — cover data breach response and
   defamation/rights claims from streaming output.
6. Equipment: cameras, streaming rig, and rink-side kit, in transit and
   at away venues.
Renewal is a standing item on the commitments calendar.
