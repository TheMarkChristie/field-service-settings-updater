# Safeguarding Policy — DRAFT for board adoption (Scotland: PVG scheme)

1. Named Safeguarding Officer appointed by the board; contact published
   to members and staff. Deputy named for absence.
2. PVG/DBS: everyone in regulated roles with under-18s (coaches, team
   staff, designated volunteers) holds a current PVG scheme membership
   (or DBS where applicable). The commitments calendar carries the
   annual review.
3. Filming and the content machine: the club films training, dressing
   rooms, and interviews (P35/P68). WHERE UNDER-18s MAY APPEAR:
   written parental consent on file before any filming; the manager
   sign-off gate for sensitive footage (platform editorial control)
   explicitly includes checking for under-18s without consent; no
   dressing-room filming of youth players, ever.
4. Match streaming: crowd shots are acceptable; deliberate close-ups of
   identifiable children require consent.
5. Contact rules: no one-to-one electronic contact between adults in
   club roles and under-18s through club channels; chat moderation
   escalates safeguarding concerns to the Safeguarding Officer, not the
   ordinary moderation ladder.
6. Concerns: any safeguarding concern goes to the Safeguarding Officer
   immediately; the officer decides on referral to Police Scotland /
   social work; records kept confidentially outside the general audit
   log.
7. Training: safeguarding basics in the volunteer handbook; formal
   training for the officer and team staff.
