# Trademarks & the Identity-Change Runbook

## Protect now
1. Register UK trademarks for the club name and badge (classes: 25
   clothing, 41 entertainment/sport, 9/38 for streaming/app at
   minimum) before merch scales. Consider EU/US if the ownership map
   warrants it.
2. The brand pack page (/brand-pack/) is the canonical asset source;
   registered marks should match the uploaded SVG master.

## When the fans vote to change the identity (75% ballot, P41)
The ballot description MUST disclose before voting opens:
- League/association ratification is required and takes [X] weeks;
- New trademark filings and the cost involved;
- Kit, signage, and merchandise transition costs and timeline;
- The platform rebrand itself is config (T46) and app-store
  resubmission takes days.
Runbook order after a passed ballot: league ratification → trademark
filing → brand pack assets updated → platform config flipped → stores
resubmitted → announcement. The decision register entry tracks each
step.
