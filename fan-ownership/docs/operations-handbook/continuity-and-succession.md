# Continuity & Succession Plan — DRAFT for board adoption

1. Bus factor: at least TWO people hold Owner-Admin on the platform,
   and at least two hold access to: registrar/DNS, hosting, Stripe,
   WooCommerce, Cloudflare, Firebase, Brevo, app store accounts, and
   the club's socials.
2. Credential vault: all club credentials live in a shared password
   manager (club-owned, not personal); named owners; quarterly access
   review on the commitments calendar. No credentials in inboxes or
   spreadsheets.
3. Club-owned accounts: every service is registered to a club email
   alias (e.g. accounts@…), never a personal address — the app stores
   especially (T68).
4. Key-person cover: for each critical role (platform admin, streaming
   lead, Governance Officer, Safeguarding Officer) a named deputy and a
   one-page handover note in the board workspace.
5. Board departures: the platform revokes access instantly on role
   removal (built); this plan adds the human checklist — bank mandate,
   Companies House, credential vault, league portal.
6. Founder scenario: if the executive lead departs, the board appoints
   an interim within 14 days and informs owners under the
   owners-hear-first protocol (P67).
