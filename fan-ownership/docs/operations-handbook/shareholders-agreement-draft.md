# Shareholders' Agreement — DRAFT (solicitor must settle before checkout opens)

Entered into by every member on purchase or redemption of shares (the
platform requires ticked acceptance at checkout and gift redemption and
keeps a versioned record of every acceptance: version, date, IP, order).
Published as a page on the club site; members can print/save a copy at
any time and are re-prompted when a new version is issued.

## 1. Parties and purpose
Between [LEGAL NAME] Ltd (the Club) and each person acquiring shares
(the Member). Purpose: to govern the relationship between the Club and
its fan owners alongside the articles of association, and to protect
the Club's reputation, community, and democratic model.

## 2. The shares
Restatement of the ownership model: tiered price ladder; 10-share cap
per Member; one vote per share in member ballots; no dividends or
financial return expected; no transfers except surrender to the Club;
transmission on death to the estate/nominated beneficiary; payment
reversal (refund/chargeback) automatically surrenders the shares that
payment acquired.

## 3. Governance
The Member agrees to the club's governance rules as published: ballot
scope and bindingness, quorum, majorities, secret ballots, eligibility
snapshots, the board's reserved matters and casting vote, and that
changes to those rules are themselves made by 75% ballot.

## 4. Conduct and morality clauses
The Member agrees that as a part-owner they carry the Club's name, and
that the Club may act (per clause 5) where a Member:
 (a) engages in violent, discriminatory, harassing, or hateful conduct
     — in club spaces (portal, app, chat, forum, matchday) or in public
     conduct that is reasonably connected to the Club;
 (b) commits a criminal offence involving violence, dishonesty, hate,
     or safeguarding harm;
 (c) brings the Club into serious disrepute, including public conduct
     or statements a reasonable person would consider gravely damaging
     to the Club's reputation or community;
 (d) attempts to manipulate club democracy: operating multiple
     accounts, buying votes, coercing other Members' ballots, or
     evading the share or gift caps;
 (e) misuses owner access: re-streaming or sharing gated content,
     leaking board-workspace material, or misusing member data;
 (f) uses the Club's marks or brand assets contrary to the brand pack
     without consent.

## 5. Sanctions and process
Graduated response mirroring the code of conduct: warning → temporary
suspension of expression/participation rights → expulsion with
surrender of shares (no payout). Safeguards: written notice of the
allegation, an opportunity to respond, decision by the board with the
outcome minuted; expulsion decisions reported (anonymised where
appropriate) in the annual report. Voting rights are only removed at
expulsion. Nothing here limits the Club's legal remedies.

## 6. The Club's obligations to Members
Honour passed ballots within their scope; publish monthly financial
summaries and annual accounts; maintain the decision register; deliver
the content commitments or account for misses; operate the platform
with the published privacy policy; never dilute holdings without a
prior member ballot on published terms.

## 7. General
Variation: new versions are published with a version number; continuing
as a Member after re-acceptance constitutes agreement; material adverse
changes go to member ballot. Governing law: Scotland [confirm].
Severability, no-partnership, entire-agreement boilerplate for the
solicitor to settle.
