# Death of a Member — Transmission Procedure (DRAFT)

Decision P30: on death, shares pass to the member's estate or their
nominated beneficiary. Members nominate on their account page ("My
nominated beneficiary"); the nomination is included in their data
export and visible to the Governance Officer on request.

## Procedure

1. **Notification** — a family member, executor, or the beneficiary
   contacts the club. The Governance Officer handles it end to end.
2. **Verification** — request a copy of the death certificate and, if
   the claimant is not the nominated beneficiary, evidence of authority
   (grant of probate / confirmation in Scotland, or executor ID).
3. **Check the nomination** — the member's `prx3_beneficiary` record.
   A nomination directs the club; without one the shares transmit to
   the estate's direction.
4. **Transmission** — record in the share register: a `surrender`
   entry for the deceased (source `transmission`) and, where the
   beneficiary is or becomes a member (18+, signs the current
   Shareholders' Agreement), a grant of the same holding to them —
   the cap still applies; any excess above their cap is surrendered.
5. **Close the account** — memorialise or close per the family's
   wishes; the register entries and acceptance log are retained as the
   legal minimum.
6. **Record** — minute the transmission (anonymised in public
   reporting); solicitor to confirm the articles' transmission clause
   matches this procedure.
