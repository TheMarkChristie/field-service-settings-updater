# Terms of Membership — DRAFT (solicitor review required before checkout opens)

These terms are presented at registration and checkout. Plain-English
summary first, legal text to be settled by the solicitor.

1. What you are buying. Ordinary shares in [LEGAL NAME] Ltd at the
   published price ladder. Maximum 10 shares per person. Each share
   carries one vote in member ballots. Shares are an ownership stake,
   not an investment product: they pay no dividend or interest, and you
   should not expect financial return.
2. Eligibility. You must be 18 or over and hold one account only.
   Accounts and holdings that evade the 10-share cap may be merged or
   the excess surrendered.
3. No refunds. Share purchases are final. Statutory rights unaffected.
4. Payment reversals. If a payment for shares is reversed (refund,
   chargeback, or dispute), the shares that payment bought are
   surrendered back to the club automatically.
5. Transfers. Shares cannot be sold or given to anyone else. They may
   be surrendered back to the club (no payout). On death, shares pass
   to your estate or nominated beneficiary.
6. Gifting. Gift purchases are capped per buyer; the recipient must
   meet these terms and redeems into their own account.
7. Conduct. The members' code of conduct applies across the portal,
   app, chat, and forum. Sanctions run warning → temporary suspension
   of posting rights → expulsion. Expulsion surrenders your shares.
8. Content. Owner content (streams, replays, meetings, documents) is
   for your personal use; sharing access or re-streaming is a breach.
9. Data. Handled per the privacy policy; your ballot choices are never
   published; the share register is retained as a legal record after
   account closure.
10. Winding up. If the club ceases to operate, remaining assets after
    creditors are distributed among shareholders per the articles; you
    may receive nothing. Buy shares because you love the club.
11. Changes. Material changes to these terms are notified to members
    and, where the governance rules require, put to ballot.
