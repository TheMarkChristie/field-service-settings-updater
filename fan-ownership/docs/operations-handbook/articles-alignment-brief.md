# Articles of Association — Alignment Brief (for the solicitor)

STATUS: DRAFT — instruct a solicitor. Nothing the platform enforces is
legally binding until the company's articles say the same thing.

## What the platform enforces (must be mirrored in the articles)

1. Share capital: ordinary shares issued at a published tiered price
   ladder; maximum 10 shares per member; one vote per share.
2. Transfers: shares transfer only back to the company (no secondary
   market); on death they transmit to the estate/nominated beneficiary;
   surrender without payout on voluntary exit or expulsion for breach of
   the members' code of conduct.
3. Member decisions ("fan-decision scope"): budget allocation, club
   identity, kits, football strategy, commercial and matchday matters,
   and confidence votes / vetted shortlists for key appointments are
   decided by member ballot and BIND the directors. Legal and
   regulatory matters are reserved to the board.
4. Ballot mechanics: electronic voting via the club's platform; secret
   until close; electorate fixed at ballot opening; quorum of 25% of
   members active in the preceding 12 months; simple majority for
   ordinary ballots; 75% supermajority for identity matters (name,
   badge, colours, relocation) and for changes to these rules
   themselves; ties resolved by a recorded board casting vote; one
   automatic re-run on failed quorum, then board decision with
   published reasoning.
5. New share issues: any allotment beyond the published pool requires a
   prior member ballot with published terms (anti-dilution promise).
6. AGM: held online; statutory resolutions may be voted electronically
   through the platform, with the platform record serving as the poll
   record.
7. Directors: chair holds a casting vote in board deliberations; a
   standing conflicts register with per-item recusal is maintained.

## Questions for the solicitor

- Best mechanism to make member ballots binding on directors (weighted
  voting rights, entrenched articles provisions, or a shareholders'
  agreement adopted by all members at purchase).
- Whether the 18+ restriction and the surrender-on-expulsion provision
  need bespoke drafting.
- Confirmation that electronic ballots satisfy Companies Act poll and
  written-resolution requirements as configured.
- The compliant route for selling shares online direct to the public
  (financial promotion rules) — see spec §9 risk 1.
