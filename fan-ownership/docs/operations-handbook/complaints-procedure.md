# Member Complaints Procedure — DRAFT for board adoption

Scope: complaints about the club (service, decisions applied to an
individual, staff conduct). Not for: content moderation appeals
(moderation ladder), ballot outcomes (governance rules), safeguarding
(safeguarding policy — immediate route).

1. Stage 1 — Informal: raise with the club by email; acknowledged in 3
   working days, answered in 10.
2. Stage 2 — Formal: unresolved complaints are put in writing and
   reviewed by a director not involved in the matter; response within
   15 working days, logged in the board workspace.
3. Stage 3 — Board review: the complainant may ask the full board to
   review; the decision and (where appropriate) an anonymised summary
   is minuted. Board decision is final within the club.
4. Reporting: complaint volumes and themes reported annually in the
   owners' report — transparency without naming individuals.
5. No detriment: making a complaint in good faith never affects a
   member's standing, shares, or access.
