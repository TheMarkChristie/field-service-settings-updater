# Board Members' Guide

Everything you can do lives in the **Board** menu.

- **Workspace** — actions awaiting you: casting votes on ties (with
  published reasoning), recommendations, reserved-matter decisions.
  Board meetings run here; papers, threads, and internal votes are
  board-only (admins entering leave a break-glass audit trail).
- **Dashboard** — the club at a glance: owners, targets, ballots,
  commerce health.
- **Decision Register** — every decision, tracked to delivery. Yours
  to keep current; stalled items are flagged.
- **Live Q&A** — the presenter view for meetings: owner questions,
  filterable to the person on stage (Board, Manager, Captain);
  ranked by upvotes, refreshing automatically.
- **Annual Report** — draft the year's report straight into the vault.
- **Identity Lookup** — a member's full identity record (birth name,
  nationality, residence, DOB, PEP declaration). The government ID is
  always masked; every view you make is permanently audited.
- **Owner Signatures** — the signed Shareholders' Agreement register.
- **Legal / Targets / Governance** — read-only for you; Owner-Admins edit.

Rules to remember: you vote in fan ballots only with shares you
personally own; your FanPress messages appear in the board colour with
a Board tag — you are always speaking as the club; running tallies on
open ballots are visible to you but never to members.
