# Volunteers' Handbook

- **Match rotas** — matchday roles live in the commitments calendar;
  your slot appears on your account page and the owners calendar feed.
- **Moderators** — FanPress Chat menu: **Held Replies** (word-filter
  holds — approve or bin), **Reports** (member reports with reasons).
  Sanctions ladder: warn → mute (timed) — applied from the member's
  screen, always audited. In live match chat you can hold, remove,
  slow-mode, and mute in place.
- **Reporters** — the reporter console in the Match Centre drives
  minute-by-minute updates and audio commentary on away days.
- **Content helpers** — Content Editor role: news, videos, match
  publishing; anything governance-flagged needs a second approver.
- **Chapter leads** — run your chapter page, watch parties, and the
  away-day threads in FanPress.

House rules: one identity everywhere, the owner gate is absolute, and
if in doubt — don't delete, hold and escalate.
