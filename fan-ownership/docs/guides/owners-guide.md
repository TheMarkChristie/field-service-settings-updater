# Owners' Guide (for members)

Welcome — you own this club. Your places:

- **Owners Hub** — your dashboard: shares, open ballots, what's on.
- **Ballots** — vote on open questions (your votes = your shares, max
  ten). Secret ballot: results appear the moment voting closes; you
  can change your vote until then. Past votes: Voting Record.
- **FanPress Chat** — the club's WhatsApp: chats by category, match
  and ballot chats appear automatically, your bubbles on the right,
  board messages in gold. Quote, pin (staff), mute chats you don't
  need, @mention people. Replies and messages email you — switch that
  off on your profile.
- **My Profile** — photo, bio, socials, up to five photos of you
  (tick the box if the club may use them), your activity ring, and
  your private identity details (only you and club compliance ever
  see them — fellow owners see only your name and nationality).
- **My Ownership** — your shares, certificate (QR-verified), signed
  agreement, gift redemption, beneficiary nomination.
- **Match Centre** — live streams, chat, minute-by-minute, replays,
  and the live player-of-the-match vote.
- **Ideas & Questions** — propose anything; 5% owner support sends an
  idea to a full ballot. Ask the club questions; upvote others'.
- **The Owners / Chapters / Bring a Fellow Fan** — the directory,
  local chapters (watch parties!), and your referral link.

One account, one owner, one community — the code of conduct applies
everywhere, and everything owner-only stays behind the gate.
