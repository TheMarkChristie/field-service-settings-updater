YouTube Cinema World

A six-screen multiplex. Give each screen its own YouTube playlist, then walk the lobby and wander into whichever room takes your fancy — you hear the screen of the room you're in.

Playlists — one per screen

Paste a playlist URL, a video URL, a channel URL (plays that channel's uploads), or a bare playlist ID. Google redirect links are unwrapped automatically. Leave a screen blank to keep that room dark. The screens come preloaded with the Scottish Summit yearly playlists — 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2026 — swap in your own any time.

While this menu is open you can also click any on-screen player directly to use YouTube's own play, captions and volume controls.

Controls
  • W A S D or arrow keys — walk
  • Shift — walk faster
  • Mouse — look around
  • Space — play / pause the room you're in
  • N / P — next / previous video in this room
  • M — mute / unmute
  • - / = — volume down / up
  • Esc — leave the walk and reopen this menu
  • Touch screens — left joystick to walk, drag anywhere else to look, on-screen buttons for playback, Menu to come back here

Single-key shortcuts are only active while you are walking (pointer locked); they are disabled when this menu is open. Audio follows you: only the room you're standing in is unmuted.

Captions: open this menu with Esc, then click the CC button on that room's player.
